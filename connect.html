Connecting to QuickBooks Online

Authorized G-Net staff only

Only authorized G-Net staff connect G-Net Accounting Connector, and only to G-Net’s own QuickBooks Online company. There is no public sign-up.

If you don’t work for G-Net, the app isn’t available to you and there’s nothing to set up.

The connection

Who connects it
Authorized G-Net staff
Company
G-Net’s own QuickBooks Online company
QuickBooks access
Accounting only. No QuickBooks Payments.
Tokens stored
Only on the G-Net staff computer that runs the app

When the connection stops working

The connection stops working if it expires, if it is disconnected from within QuickBooks Online, or if G-Net revokes its tokens.

How staff reconnect

  1. An authorized G-Net staff member signs in to QuickBooks Online on Intuit’s own sign-in screen.
  2. Intuit shows the access the app is asking for, QuickBooks Online Accounting only, and the staff member approves it for G-Net’s company.
  3. The new authorization tokens are saved on that staff member’s computer, where only they can access them.

Reconnecting doesn’t change how the app handles data. See the Privacy Policy.
