This policy explains how G-Net Facility Solutions, Inc. (“G-Net”, “we”) handles data in G-Net Accounting Connector (the “app”) and on this website.

1. About the app

G-Net Accounting Connector is a private, internal tool. It is Intuit’s open-source QuickBooks Online MCP server, run on a G-Net staff computer and used with an AI assistant, Claude, made by Anthropic. It connects only to G-Net’s own QuickBooks Online company.

The app isn’t offered, sold or licensed to anyone else, and there is no public sign-up.

2. Data the app can access

The app asks QuickBooks Online for Accounting access only, using the scope com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting. It does not request QuickBooks Payments access.

With that access, it reads G-Net’s own accounting data as needed for bookkeeping:

  • the chart of accounts
  • customers and vendors
  • invoices and other transactions
  • reports

3. How the data is used

The data is used only for G-Net’s own bookkeeping. The app reads what a G-Net staff member’s request needs, and it makes changes in QuickBooks only when a G-Net staff member asks it to.

4. Who receives the data

To process each request, the data the AI assistant reads is sent to Anthropic, the company that provides Claude. Anthropic handles it under its own terms and privacy policy.

G-Net does not sell, rent or share the data with anyone else.

5. Where data is kept, and for how long

  • In QuickBooks Online. G-Net’s accounting records stay in G-Net’s QuickBooks Online company.
  • On the staff computer. The AI assistant’s chat history, and any reports or files a staff member saves, can contain copies of QuickBooks data. These copies are kept only on the one G-Net staff computer that runs the app, under that staff member’s account, until they are deleted.
  • With Anthropic. Anthropic handles the data sent to it under its own terms and privacy policy.

6. Authorization tokens

The app connects to QuickBooks Online with authorization tokens issued by Intuit. The tokens are stored only on that G-Net staff computer, and only that staff member can access them. They stop working when the app is disconnected or G-Net revokes them.

7. Revoking access

The app can be disconnected from within QuickBooks Online at any time, and G-Net can also revoke its tokens. Either way, the app can no longer access G-Net’s QuickBooks Online company.

See Disconnected from QuickBooks Online and Connecting to QuickBooks Online.

8. This website

This website collects no personal data. It has no forms, cookies, analytics or trackers, and it doesn’t load anything from other websites.

It is hosted on GitHub Pages. When GitHub serves these pages, it may log basic technical data, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

9. Third-party services

QuickBooks Online is provided by Intuit, and Claude by Anthropic. Their own terms and privacy policies also apply to their services:

  • Intuit Global Privacy Statement
  • Anthropic Privacy Policy

G-Net may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the bottom of this page shows when it last changed.
