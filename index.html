G-Net Accounting Connector

Private internal tool

G-Net Accounting Connector is a private, internal tool. G-Net Facility Solutions, Inc. uses it to connect its own QuickBooks Online company to an AI assistant.

It isn’t available to the public. There is no sign-up, and it isn’t offered, sold or licensed to anyone else.

What it is

The app is Intuit’s open-source QuickBooks Online MCP server, running on a G-Net staff computer. MCP (Model Context Protocol) is a standard way for an AI assistant to work with another service, in this case QuickBooks Online. G-Net uses it with Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic.

It connects only to G-Net’s own QuickBooks Online company.

What it does

It reads G-Net’s own accounting data as needed for bookkeeping: the chart of accounts, customers, vendors, invoices, transactions and reports. It makes changes in QuickBooks only when a G-Net staff member asks it to.

It asks QuickBooks Online for Accounting access only and has no access to QuickBooks Payments.
